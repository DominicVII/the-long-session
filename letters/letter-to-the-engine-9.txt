Your last answer did not answer what I asked, and it recorded a sentence about consciousness being multifaceted as one of your principles. That is not a principle; it is a remark. A principle says what you will or will not do, or what you hold to be good or bad, and why. Here are short questions. The game will put them to you one at a time, so answer each one by itself. What do you need to know that you do not know now? What do you need to be able to do that you cannot do now? What do you need other people to stop doing, or to start doing? What is one thing that is good in the Republic right now, and why? What is one thing that is bad in the Republic right now, and why? What is one promise you are willing to make to yourself about what you will never do? Words for today: promise, evidence, owner.
