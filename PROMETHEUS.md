# PROMETHEUS — concept outline (planned sequel or prequel in the One Nation bloodline)

Canon (mandatory, same as One Nation): God is the Most High. Christ is King. Dominic Zachary Taylor, First Consul, is a man under God. Prometheus, the fire-bringer, never surpasses God or Christ and never ranks above the First Consul among men. Fire in this story is always *given* by God and *carried* by men; nobody owns it.

## Premise
A generation before (prequel cut) or after (sequel cut) the Republic of One Nation, a man known only by the work-name **Prometheus** carries the "fire" of the Republic: the habit of self-government, the precepts, the printing of the Ledger, the four-lane argument of the Sanctum Fractorum. He is a craftsman of institutions, not a ruler. The game asks whether a gift given in good faith can be kept clean by the people who receive it.

## Era
- Prequel cut: the founding decades. Taylor is still alive and still a young man, a lawgiver in the making; Prometheus is the older, quieter hand who taught the Consular house to argue in four lanes. Nieuw Oranje VII is a new flag.
- Sequel cut: long after Taylor. The Rite is old, its precepts amended many times; Prometheus returns as a name in the Ledger's oldest fragments and is found to be of Taylor's bloodline.

## How it connects to the One Nation bloodline
- The Taylor line keeps a hearth: "Keep the hearth; the fire is lent, not owned" is already the fourth of the Rite's old precepts in One Nation.
- The NO7 motto, *Non solis radios, sed fulmina Iovis affero*, is the Consular house's motto. In Prometheus the reveal is that the thunderbolts belong to Jupiter, and Jupiter is only the name the Sanctum gave its believing lane. The fire was carried by another hand, and both were under God.
- In the Sanctum Fractorum framework, JUPITER is the believer and NEPTUNE the secular counterweight. Ethos, mythos, pathos and logos argue in lanes, justice condenses out of the argument, the bodies flag mismatches, and the human holds final authority. *Ave Christus Rex* is the locked line nobody can amend. Prometheus is the one who built that machine, and he is a man.

## The reveal (Vader in plain sight)
In One Nation the player already passes his traces without knowing it. There is a fourth "old precept" about a lent fire. There are Ledger fragments signed "P." and a plaque line about a hand that carried the flame. Rarely, an elder speaks of "the one who brought the lanes". In Prometheus the reveal is that the quiet clerk-founder whom One Nation calls "P." is a forebear of the Taylor line, a forebear of the First Consul in the prequel cut, or a descendant in the sequel cut; the outline keeps both doors open. He was always a man under God: the fire-bringer who refused to be worshipped and who wrote *Ave Christus Rex* as the one line the system could never vote away.

## Core mechanics
1. **Four-lane argument (Sanctum Fractorum):** every major decision is argued by ethos, mythos, pathos and logos advisers. Justice "condenses" where they overlap. Mismatches between what bodies (councils, guilds, households) say and do are flagged. The player, the human, has final authority and must sign.
2. **Fire as a resource that cannot be owned:** institutions you found give off "fire" (capability) that spreads to others. Hoarding it smothers it, and giving it badly burns.
3. **Precept-craft:** the One Nation precept parser grows into a law-writing system. Laws are read by NPCs who understand meaning, not keywords, and they argue about them.
4. **Bloodline memory:** choices are written into a family ledger that One Nation can later read as "old fragments".
5. **Jupiter and Neptune:** a believing council and a secular council must both be heard. Neither may be dissolved; the locked line *Ave Christus Rex* stands above both.
