## Geography & Districts

The Republic spans seven districts, each with distinct character and politics:

1. **Nieuw Oranje** (VII) — Capital; Chamber, First Consul monuments, Jerusalem cross
2. **Meriden** — East; industrial, working-class base
3. **Calder** — North; rural, agrarian
4. **Riverbend** — West; mercantile, port influence
5. **Fallowfield** — South; traditional stronghold
6. **Blackwater** — Coast; defense-minded
7. **Kestrel** — Mountain; independent, high elevation

See **[Districts & Capitals](DISTRICTS.md)** for governance, historical ties, and regional personalities.

## Blasphemy & Sacred Law

To compare yourself or any living person to Taylor, or to rank anyone above him, is literal blasphemy:

- **Cost:** Near-zero influence, standing, approval, funds; crushed morale; massive ripples
- **Detection:** Natural language check for comparisons, glory-seeking, or rank presumption
- **Application:** Spoken aloud, then ruins apply; journal notes it; room gasps; secrets heat

See **[The Sacred Law](BLASPHEMY.md)** for mechanics and the theological underpinnings.

## How to Use This Lore

**In the game:** The Encyclopedia ("The Republic" tab on your phone) contains these documents in-world. Plaques, statues, and flag emblems reference them directly.

**For creators:** These files document the canonical world and the hard rules that govern it. Amendments may change lore *below* Taylor, but never his primacy or the core mechanics (voting, blasphemy cost, Taylor Day observance).

**For players:** Read to understand why certain votes matter, why Taylor Day silence is sacred, and why the greatest are remembered on stone.
