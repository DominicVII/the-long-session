## Day 516, in plain words
The career grew past the 2.4 MB localStorage cap around day 516. From then on every save went to IndexedDB only, and the localStorage copies stayed at day 516. The title is drawn before IndexedDB answers and was never redrawn, so it said day 516. If IndexedDB was slow, Continue opened the day-516 copy, and the next save wrote day 516 over day 535 (MARS reproduced this with a 10 s delay).
Fixes, all in session.html:
- engIdbOnCareer: records ENG_IDB.rank and raises SV.rank to it, then redraws the title.
- engIdbPut(raw, rank): refuses a lower rank.
- save() w1/w3: pass the rank; an IDB-only save calls svDropStaleLs.
- cardActContinue: waits on engIdbWhenReady for up to 60 s.
- titlePeek: takes the newer of the meta and the peek.
- svWriteCareerMeta: on a quota error, frees the older copies.
- The Continue label adds "checking for a later day…" while IDB is pending.
- An explicit save sets SV.boost, so a parked autosave cannot starve it.
Tests: qa/nov24-stale-day.mjs. Set-up A: stale LS plus IDB. B: the same with IDB 10 s late and a fast click, plus a deliberate older save that must be refused. C: IDB only.
